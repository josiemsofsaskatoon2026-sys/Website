About Me

My name is Josilene Finga Zawadi Kashindi, but most people know me as Josie. I was born in Uvira, Democratic Republic of Congo, where I spent my early childhood surrounded by a strong sense of family, culture, and resilience.

At the age of nine, my life took a new direction when my family and I moved to Nairobi, Kenya, where I experienced both transition and growth. During this time, by the grace of God, my brother adopted me, providing stability and support as we adjusted to a new way of life.

We were later given the opportunity to rebuild our lives in Calgary, Alberta, Canada, a moment that marked a powerful turning point in my journey. Canada became a place of healing, hope, and new beginnings, and I am deeply proud to call it home.

As I continued to grow, I developed a deep appreciation for the peace and beauty of the prairies, which eventually led me to Saskatoon, Saskatchewan—the Land of Living Skies—where I now proudly reside. I enjoy research, reading, learning, meeting new people, gardening, road trips, long walks, and embracing life’s simple joys.

My journey across continents has shaped me into a woman of faith, resilience, and purpose. I am a devoted mother, a business sales professional, an aspiring runway model, and the proud Ms. of Saskatoon 2026. I also serve on the Refuge Advisory Table and volunteer with various organizations and non-profits, dedicating my time to community service, newcomer support, and initiatives that uplift vulnerable and marginalized groups. Above all, I am committed to being a voice for empowerment, inclusion, and community building.

In alignment with this mission, I am the founder of the Amani Congo Cultural Community of Saskatchewan, where I work to bridge the integration gap for visible minorities and support newcomers in building meaningful lives in Canada, particularly within the Congolese community. My passion lies in creating spaces where people from all backgrounds feel seen, valued, supported, and inspired to thrive.

I aspire to inspire young girls to believe in themselves, embrace their unique beauty, and recognize the strength within their abilities and skills.

I stand for unity, strength, and compassion, and I believe true beauty shines brightest when it uplifts others and helps build communities where everyone belongs.
